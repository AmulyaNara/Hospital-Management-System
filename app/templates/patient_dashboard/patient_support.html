Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions.

How can I download my medical reports?

Navigate to the Records or Lab Results page, select the required report and click the Download button.

How do I reschedule an appointment?

Open the Appointments page, choose your appointment, and click the Reschedule button.

Why is my insurance claim still pending?

Insurance verification may take 5–10 business days depending on your insurance provider.

Can I pay my hospital bill online?

Yes. Visit the Billing page and choose the available online payment option.

How do I contact my doctor?

Visit your Appointments page or contact hospital reception.
